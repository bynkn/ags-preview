The realisation

Problem: your agent acts, and nobody sees it

It reads files. Calls APIs. Queries databases. Runs commands.

It drifts. It makes things up. It gets weaponized. Then it acts, at machine speed.

And you’ll never know it happened. Until it’s too late.

No breach is required. An agent that was never attacked can still misread its instructions and act with complete confidence on a wrong conclusion.

Today’s tools read prompts, policies or outputs, or find out afterwards. None of them stop the action before it runs.

Who feels it most

SMEs. They are adopting AI fast, often without a dedicated AI security team or enterprise-grade protection.

What’s at stake

Harm at machine speed in payments, software development, personnel data, legal operations and health administration, before a human can step in. And regulatory exposure: agents can trigger unintended breaches of GDPR, the EU AI Act and contractual obligations.

Why now

Everyone wants agents. Almost nobody trusts them yet. That gap is where Agentic Guardians lives.

The industry answered “how do I build this agent?” Nobody answered “how do I make sure it’s safe?”

As companies hand work to agents, they remove the people who would have caught a rogue one. Agentic Guardians is the oversight layer that’s left.

It needs to exist before the first serious incident, not after.

The answer

Product: now you’ll know what it’s doing

Agentic Guardians learns what your agent normally does. It checks every action before it runs. When something doesn’t fit, it stops it, or pauses it for a human. Before the damage, not after.

A payments agent always runs read_file → match_ledger → write_report.

One day it tries an http.post to an account it has never paid.

Blocked before it ran. Nothing sent. A signed certificate records why.

What it lets you know

  • Every action was checked before it ran. Not logged afterwards.
  • No prompt can talk it round. It never reads prompts, so it can’t be injected or persuaded.
  • Your data stayed with you. Stays local. No cloud round-trip.
  • Nothing heavy to run. On CPU. No GPU needed.
  • Nothing to configure. No rules to write. It learns what normal looks like.

Proof

Every block is signed (Ed25519). Anyone can verify it.

Each block leaves a Detection Certificate: what was stopped, and why. You don’t have to take our word for it. Neither do your auditors.

How it works

  1. Learn. Builds each agent’s behavioural fingerprint from its normal action sequences.
  2. Check. Scores each next action before it runs.
  3. Act. Allows it, pauses it for review, or blocks it.
  4. Prove. Signs every block.

It watches actions (file reads, API calls, shell commands, database queries), not prompts or tokens. The engine is a statistical behavioural model with no language model in the decision path.

How it’s different

Not AI watching AI.

AI guarding AI puts the lock and the thief in the same paradigm. A prompt that reaches the agent can reach the guard. You cannot ask a corrupted process to report its own corruption.

Agentic Guardians works outside the language layer. It reads what the agent does, never what it’s told.

  • Prompt filters and guardrails read language, so they share the model’s attack surface.
  • Logs and monitoring find problems after the fact.
  • Manual review can’t keep up with machine speed.
  • AI supervising AI adds another layer that can itself be fooled.

Lightweight enough for SMEs. Robust enough for regulated workflows. Built on peer-reviewed research into behavioural sequence models (IEEE Access, 2020), proven in production fraud detection before being pointed at AI agents.

Alignment

Agentic Guardians is designed to align with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF), ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001 and GDPR. Signed blocks and pause-for-review support auditability, human oversight and compliance evidence.

Stage

Private beta. The engine works end to end: per-agent baselines, blocks before execution, signed certificates. We’re onboarding our first teams personally.
