The realisation

Problem: your agent acts, and nobody sees it

Your agent reads files, calls APIs, queries databases and runs commands. On its own, at machine speed.

It drifts. It makes things up. It gets weaponized. Then it acts.

A compromised agent doesn’t look compromised. It isn’t a break-in. It’s a takeover from the inside. And you’ll never know it happened. Until it’s too late.

It doesn’t even take an attack. Sometimes the agent just decides.

Today’s tools read prompts, check policies, or find out afterwards. None of them stop the action before it runs.

Who feels it most

Small and mid-sized companies. They’re adopting AI fast, often without a security team to watch it.

What’s at stake

Mistakes in payments, code, personnel records, legal work and health administration, made faster than a person can step in. Some of them break GDPR, the EU AI Act or a contract.

Why now

Every major AI company is racing to make agents deployable at scale. The thing blocking adoption is trust.

Everyone wants agents. Almost nobody trusts them yet. And as companies hand work to agents, they lose the people who would have caught a rogue one.

Nobody has solved trust with maths yet. We’ve spent over a decade on the maths it needs.

The window is now.

The answer

Product: now you’ll know what it’s doing

Every agent has a rhythm. Agentic Guardians learns it. When the rhythm breaks, the action stops before it runs.

If an action fits, it goes through. If it doesn’t, Agentic Guardians blocks it, or pauses it for a person to look at.

A payments agent always runs read_file → match_ledger → write_report.

One day it tries an http.post to an account it has never paid.

Blocked before it ran. Nothing sent. A signed certificate records why.

What that gives you

  • Every action checked before it runs. Not logged after.
  • Nothing to persuade. It never reads prompts, so it can’t be injected.
  • Your data stays with you. Stays local. No cloud round-trip.
  • Nothing heavy to run. On CPU. No GPU needed.
  • No rules to write. It learns what normal looks like.

Proof

Every block is signed (Ed25519). Anyone can verify it.

You don’t need to understand the maths. You just need to check the signature.

Each block leaves a Detection Certificate: what was stopped, and why. You don’t have to take our word for it. Neither do your auditors.

How it works

  1. Learn. It watches an agent’s actions and learns its normal sequences.
  2. Check. Before each action runs, it scores it against everything that came before.
  3. Act. Allow, pause, or block.
  4. Prove. Sign every block.

It reads actions: file reads, API calls, shell commands, database queries. Never prompts. The engine is a statistical behavioural model. No language model in the decision path.

Why we win: not AI watching AI

Who guards the guardians?

Quis custodiet ipsos custodes. Juvenal, 1st century AD.

Most tools that watch agents are AI themselves. Same architecture. Same attack surface. They speak the same language as the agent, so a prompt injection that fools the agent can reach the guard too.

You cannot ask a corrupted process to report its own corruption. An AI watching an AI is just another alignment problem.

Our engine doesn’t speak that language. It doesn’t process tokens. It doesn’t read prompts, so a prompt can’t reach it. There’s nothing for an injection to talk to.

It watches sequences of actions at the system level. Statistical models, not language models. Patterns in what the agent does, not meaning in what it’s told. CPU, not GPU.

A compromised agent can say all the right things. It can’t fake its rhythm.

Filters read the attacker’s language. Logs tell you afterwards. People can’t keep up with machine speed. Agentic Guardians stands outside all of that, and an agent can’t talk its way past something that never listens.

That’s why we win.

Alignment

We designed Agentic Guardians to align with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF), ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001 and GDPR. Signed blocks give auditors a record. Pause-for-review keeps a person in the loop.

Stage

Private beta. The engine works end to end: it learns each agent’s baseline, blocks before execution, and signs every block. We’re onboarding our first teams ourselves.
