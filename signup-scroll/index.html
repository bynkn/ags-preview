What exists today

Today’s tools check who an agent is, or what it says.

  • Access checks verify credentials, not intent.
  • Prompt filters read language, so language can fool them.
  • Logs and reviews tell you afterwards.
  • Another AI as guard can be misled by the same prompt.

How it works

It watches what your agent does. Not what it’s told.

Runs locally on any CPU. No language model. Light enough for a small team.
